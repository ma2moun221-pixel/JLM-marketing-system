# Engine Pulse — نبض المحرّك

Design philosophy for the JLM 20-second product film, written with the
`canvas-design` skill (philosophy first, then canvas) and extended to motion with
the `algorithmic-art` skill (a seeded flow field instead of a static pattern).

## Movement: "Liquid Precision"

The machine is dark and the oil is light. Every frame starts in graphite, the
colour of a warm engine bay at night, and lets one material move through it: a
thin amber-gold stream that behaves like oil under pressure. It never splashes or
decorates. It flows, pulses and wipes. It is the only living thing on the canvas,
and every cut follows it.

Colour is rationed with the discipline of a precision part. Graphite `#0D0E10`
and steel `#23262B` carry the frame. Silver `#C9CDD2` and white carry the
product and the type. JLM gold `#C8A165`, the same bronze printed on the
"Premium Grade" label, is kept for the one word or line that has to be read
first. Nothing else gets colour. When gold appears, it means something.

Space follows the product. Each product stands alone in the centre of the frame
with room around it, lit from above and to one side. A soft reflection sits
beneath it, as if it stands on polished black stone. Behind it, its name is set
huge and almost invisible as a watermark: architecture, not information. The
Arabic message sits in the lower third in Helvetica Neue LT Arabic (JLM's own
typeface), short enough to read in one breath, and stays on screen long enough
to be read twice.

Rhythm comes from a heartbeat. The film opens on a pulse line, the engine's
heartbeat, and each product arrives on a beat: one product, one promise, one
pulse, every three seconds. Motion eases in and out like a well-oiled piston,
never linear and never bouncing. The light sweep that crosses each product is
timed to the frame.

The film should look meticulously crafted, the result of countless refinements
by someone at the top of automotive advertising. Every margin is measured, every
fade weighted and every line of type kept inside the safe areas of Reels and
TikTok. Polish comes from refining what is already there, not from adding more.
Restraint is how the film shows the product is premium.

## The quiet reference

The pulse line is an ECG trace that is also an oil-pressure gauge needle at
idle. Mechanics will recognise the gauge, and everyone else will feel the
heartbeat. The hook line, "محرّكك قلب سيارتك", makes the reference explicit
only once.
