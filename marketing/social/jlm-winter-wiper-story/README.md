# JLM winter story — «البرد ما بوقّفك!» (1080×1920)

    python3 scripts/build.py      # → out/jlm_winter_wiper_story.png

**The idea:** the story is a car windshield covered in frost. The wiper has cleared an arc from the
bottom, and through it the viewer sees a warm night with three JLM winter products on a wet floor.
The slogan sits on the frost in charcoal with a yellow tag. The background is light, so the logo is the
official charcoal one (`logo_charcoal_on_white_src.png`, with no box).

**Products:** the three come from the winter campaign. Each line is taken from its label only.

| Product | Line |
|---|---|
| 5W-30 Oil | «زيت محرك بلزوجة 5W-30» |
| Penetrating Oil | «يذيب الصدأ ويطرد الرطوبة» |
| Fuel System Cleaner (petrol) | «ينظّف البخاخات بعمق» |

**CTA:** «اطلبها الآن من خلال» jlmstore.com · «مع توفر خدمة التوصيل». It sits above the bottom
≈ 230 px (the Stories UI).
