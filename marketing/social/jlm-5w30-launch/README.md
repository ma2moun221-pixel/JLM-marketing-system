# JLM 5W-30 «launch» post (1080×1350)

The client liked an idea from another brand's ad (an oil bottle taking off like a rocket beside a curved
ramp). This is our own image built from scratch on that idea — no stock or third-party photo is used:
a night runway, a curved concrete ramp with JLM-yellow LED edges and a steel truss, the JLM 5W-30 bottle
(client photo, cut out) tilted for take-off with a warm rim light, four thrust jets and a smoke plume lit
from inside. Copy in the JLM fonts: «انطلق بأداء أعلى» / «زيت محرك JLM 5W-30», the logo, jlmstore.com.
